How Omnivera works

Three steps turn scattered exports into one verified record.

This is the actual sequence every record passes through — not a marketing device. Nothing is marked verified until it clears all three.

01 — CONNECT

Pull from source, natively

Read-only connectors for your CRM, billing, support desk, and spreadsheets. No exports, no CSV relay races.

02 — RECONCILE

Match records across systems

Omnivera matches entities across tools, flags mismatches, and shows you exactly where two systems disagree and why.

03 — VERIFY

Stamp the resolved record

Once mismatches are resolved or explained, the record is sealed with a timestamp and confidence score your team can cite.
