About Omnivera Pvt Ltd

We got tired of arguing about whose number was right.

Omnivera started as a shared spreadsheet three founders kept correcting by hand. It's now the layer that stops that correction from being manual — or necessary.

FOUNDED 2023, HYDERABAD 14 PEOPLE, 3 TIME ZONES BOOTSTRAPPED THROUGH SEED PRIVATE LIMITED COMPANY

Origin

A short, true history.

No invented decades of legacy — Omnivera is young, and we'd rather show the real path than dress it up.

LATE 2022

The spreadsheet problem

Our founders were running ops at three different startups, independently maintaining the same kind of "master" spreadsheet to catch what their tools disagreed on.

EARLY 2023

Omnivera incorporated

Registered as Omnivera Pvt Ltd with a single goal: make reconciliation a background process, not a Friday-afternoon ritual.

MID 2023

First verified record

Shipped the first version of the reconciliation engine to three design-partner teams, matching CRM and billing data automatically.

2024

The seal

Introduced confidence-scored, timestamped "verified records" — the idea that's now central to how the product looks and works.

TODAY

Rolling out early access

A small, deliberately limited group of teams are on Omnivera now, while we harden the platform for a wider release.

How we work

Three things we don't compromise on.

Not a poster on the wall — the rules that shape what we build and say no to.

Show the disagreement

We never quietly pick a winning number. If two systems disagree, the product says so — and shows both.

Read-only until asked

Omnivera reads your systems by default. It only ever writes back when a team explicitly turns that on.

Confidence, not false certainty

A verified record still carries a score. We'd rather say "97% sure" than round up to a comforting 100.
